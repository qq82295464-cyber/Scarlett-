某制造企业：技术资料解析 AI POC 方案A manufacturer: AI POC for parsing technical documents

行业Industry
制造业（含外贸订单业务）Manufacturing, with export orders
问题Problem
复杂组合线的图纸和技术资料层级多、格式不统一，报价和下单前都要靠人工拆解。企业想先用小样本验证：AI 能不能把最难的技术资料变成报价、下单真正能用的结构化数据。Drawings and technical documents for complex assembly lines are multi-level and non-standard, so quoting and ordering depend on manual breakdown. The goal: prove on a small sample that AI can turn the hardest documents into structured data that quoting and ordering can actually use.
方案Approach
不重建 CRM、ERP、MES，只在现有数据底座上加一层 AI 工作台。主 POC 做技术资料解析，辅验证用 20–30 封脱敏海外邮件离线解析订单，暂不写入 CRM。数据安全走两条路线：默认脱敏 API 先行，同步评估专用推理或本地模型。划三道红线：不自动对外报价、不做原产地和关税最终判定、不承诺降本百分比。驻场方式是 1 名 FDE 与企业人员结对，分阶段从 POC 走到小试点。No rebuilding of CRM, ERP or MES; add an AI workbench on top of the existing data layer. The main POC parses technical documents; a side test parses 20–30 anonymized overseas order emails offline, without writing to CRM. Two data-security routes: anonymized API first, with dedicated or on-premise inference evaluated in parallel. Three red lines: no automatic quotes to customers, no final origin or tariff rulings, no promised cost-saving percentage. One FDE pairs on-site with company staff, moving in stages from POC to a small pilot.
计划产出Planned output
可追溯来源、经人工确认、能直接接入报价的结构化数据，以及 POC 验收报告。方案设定的验收目标：关键字段准确率不低于 85%，来源 100% 可追溯。Traceable, human-confirmed structured data that feeds quoting, plus a POC acceptance report. Acceptance targets in the plan: at least 85% accuracy on key fields and 100% source traceability.

某大型制造企业：内部 AI 应用创新大赛暨数字员工共创计划A large manufacturer: internal AI innovation contest and digital-employee co-creation program

行业Industry
制造业，已有专业数字化团队和企业大学Manufacturing, with an in-house IT team and corporate university
问题Problem
各岗位里分散着大量效率损耗和改进机会，但员工不清楚 AI 和本职工作的关系，好的场景很难被系统地发现和孵化。Efficiency losses and improvement ideas are scattered across roles, but staff don't see how AI relates to their work, so good use cases are hard to surface and incubate.
方案Approach
4 周轻量化赛制：一场启动、一场集中实训、两次线上辅导、一场决赛。设两个赛道：AI 应用创意组和数字员工实践组。评审看业务价值、效果验证、安全可靠和推广潜力，数据泄露、越权连接业务系统、伪造验证结果一票否决。涉及配方、工艺参数和生产控制的应用须专项审批，首届不自动执行关键业务操作。机制参考了多家大型企业公开的赛事做法，规模从简。A light 4-week format: one kickoff, one hands-on training day, two online coaching sessions, one final. Two tracks: AI application ideas and digital-employee prototypes. Judging covers business value, verified results, safety and reuse potential; data leaks, unauthorized system connections or faked results disqualify outright. Anything touching formulas, process parameters or production control needs separate approval. The format borrows from large companies' public contests, scaled down.
计划产出Planned output
项目进行中，以下为方案规划：建议 50–100 名员工参与，沉淀 AI 场景储备库、应用原型、人才名单和赛事总结报告。优秀作品进入试点候选，是否部署由企业另行验证决定，不预设部署数量。In progress; per the plan, 50–100 staff are suggested; outputs are a use-case library, prototypes, a talent list and a summary report. Strong entries become pilot candidates; deployment is decided separately, with no preset number.

自研 SkillOwn skill

产业链智能拓客 SkillIndustry-chain prospecting skill

解决什么What it solves
做 AI 售前时，常常要从一家龙头企业或一个产业集群出发，找出它的上下游、配套企业和潜在客户，再判断谁最值得先接触、哪里有 AI 自动化机会。靠人工检索既慢，又容易把猜测当成事实。AI pre-sales often starts from one industry leader or cluster: find its suppliers, partners and potential clients, then decide who to approach first and where AI automation fits. Manual research is slow and easily mistakes guesses for facts.
怎么工作How it works
这是我自己写的 AI 技能，让 AI 按固定流程做研究：界定范围、核实种子企业、绘制产业链、筛选候选企业、识别 AI 机会、评分排序、制定拓客方案、列出证据与限制。它有几条硬规矩：每条结论都要附来源链接和检索日期；同处一个园区或名单不算供应关系；事实、推断、假设和未知分开标注；没有直接证据的 AI 机会只能写成「机会假设」；联系人只用官方公开渠道，不代发消息。A skill I wrote that has AI follow a fixed research flow: scope, verify the seed company, map the chain, screen candidates, spot AI opportunities, score and rank, plan outreach, list evidence and limits. Hard rules: every claim carries a source link and date; sharing a park or a list is not a supply relationship; facts, inferences, assumptions and unknowns are labeled separately; AI opportunities without direct evidence stay "hypotheses"; contacts come only from official public channels, and nothing is sent on the user's behalf.
产出什么What it produces
一份可复查的研究与拓客报告：范围说明、结论摘要、产业链结构、候选客户排序、AI 机会卡片、行动计划和来源清单。目前是 V1.0，配有方法说明、评分规则、来源核验规范和报告模板，已用它做过一份样例报告，还在持续迭代。A checkable research and outreach report: scope, summary, chain map, ranked candidates, AI opportunity cards, action plan and sources. It is at V1.0 with a method guide, scoring rules, source-check rules and a report template, has produced one sample report, and is still evolving.

超级大脑：多 Agent 共享知识库Superbrain: a shared knowledge base for many agents

问题Problem
同时用好几个 AI 助理，每个都要重新介绍一遍自己和项目，经验散落在各个对话里。Working with several AI assistants meant re-explaining myself and every project to each one, with lessons scattered across chats.
做法Approach
用 GitHub 私有仓库加 Obsidian 搭一个 Markdown 知识库作为唯一事实来源，按项目、领域、资源、归档分类。所有 Agent 从同一份规则文件进入，按固定顺序读，干完活自动回写复盘和可复用技能；客户资料默认脱敏。A Markdown knowledge base in a private GitHub repo plus Obsidian, used as the single source of truth and organized by projects, areas, resources and archives. Every agent enters through one rules file, reads in a fixed order, and writes back reviews and reusable skills after each task. Client material is anonymized by default.
结果Result
Codex、Cursor、Grok Bot 等多个 Agent 已接入，统一读写同一个大脑。模型可以换，大脑留下来。Codex, Cursor, Grok Bot and other agents now read and write the same brain. Models can change; the brain stays.

方法论Method

我做 GEO 的方法How I approach GEO

  1. 整理目标用户会怎么问 AI，按真实口语问法建一个问题库，而不是堆关键词。Collect how real people phrase questions to AI and build a question bank, instead of stuffing keywords.
  2. 统一品牌名称、业务描述和服务范围，让全网只有一个版本。Keep the brand name, description and scope identical everywhere.
  3. 把内容写成问答、对照表和分点短句，让 AI 容易读懂和摘取。Write in Q&A, comparison tables and short points that AI can parse and quote.
  4. 补齐真实的资质、案例和口碑，不造假；虚假信息只会被 AI 降权。Back it with real credentials, cases and reviews; anything fake gets downranked.
  5. 在 AI 常引用的平台分发，用第一人称讲真实经历，定期更新并复查效果。Publish where AI tends to cite, tell real first-person stories, update regularly and recheck.
